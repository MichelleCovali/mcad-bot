MCAD collects reports of cybersecurity incidents affecting vessels, ports, maritime organisations, offshore facilities, and related systems. Reports may describe confirmed or suspected incidents, but their uncertainty must be clearly recorded. General threats, hypothetical attacks and ordinary physical attacks without a cyber or electronic-navigation component are excluded.

## Include

Collect reports describing an actual or suspected cybersecurity
incident affecting:

- Vessels and onboard IT or operational systems.
- Ports, terminals and maritime logistics.
- Shipping companies, shipyards and maritime organisations.
- Offshore facilities and their supporting systems.
- Maritime navigation, AIS/GNSS or satellite communications.

Keep suspected incidents clearly labelled as suspected.
Being relevant does not mean the incident is confirmed.

## Exclude

- General cybersecurity advice, marketing and threat predictions.
- Vulnerabilities or demonstrations without a reported real incident.
- Ordinary physical attacks without a reported cyber or
  electronic-navigation component.
- Incidents without an identifiable maritime connection.

## Needs human review

- Technical failures with no established malicious cause.
- AIS gaps or deliberate AIS disabling without evidence of compromise.
- Claims made only by attackers or other uncorroborated sources.
- Reports with conflicting explanations or unclear maritime impact.

Do not turn suspicion into confirmation.
Record what is reported, what supports it and what remains unknown.

## New incidents, updates and duplicates

- New incident: a separate event not already recorded.
- Material update: new evidence that changes an existing incident's
  known facts, confirmation status, affected systems or impact.
- Duplicate: another report repeating known information without
  a meaningful change.

Link updates to the existing incident instead of creating a new one.
Keep useful additional sources even when they describe the same event.

## Research modes

- Recent discovery: find newly reported incidents and material updates.
- Historical research: investigate older incidents and client-provided
  leads that may be missing from MCAD.

The recent-reporting time limit does not apply to historical research.

## Publication

All collected leads require human review before publication.
Unknown facts remain blank.
These rules are a draft pending client confirmation.
